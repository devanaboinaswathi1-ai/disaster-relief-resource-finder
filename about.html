About Disaster Relief Resource Finder

The Disaster Relief Resource Finder is a comprehensive, real-time platform designed to help citizens quickly locate essential relief resources during natural disasters and emergencies across Andhra Pradesh and other major cities in India.

Our mission is to save lives by providing instant access to critical information about shelters, medical facilities, food distribution centers, and water supply points during times of crisis.

Resource Types

🏠 Shelter Centers

Emergency accommodation facilities including municipal relief camps, community halls, stadiums, and government buildings equipped with beds, blankets, and basic amenities.

🏥 Medical Facilities

Government hospitals, primary health centers, mobile medical units, and Red Cross centers providing emergency care, medicines, ambulance services, and specialized treatment.

🍲 Food Distribution Centers

Anna Canteens, community kitchens, NGO food distribution points, and government relief centers offering free or subsidized meals, packaged food, and emergency rations.

💧 Water Supply Points

Municipal water supply centers, treatment plants, water tanker distribution points, and emergency water purification stations ensuring access to clean drinking water.

How to Use

  1. Select Location: Choose your city from the dropdown or use "Use My Location" button
  2. Filter by Type: Select the type of resource you need (shelter, food, medical, water)
  3. Set Radius: Adjust the search radius (1-100 km) to find nearby centers
  4. Click Search: View results on the interactive map and in the list panel
  5. Get Details: Click on markers or list items to see complete information including address, phone, hours, and available supplies
